Notes: Mather offered the book at a New York trade sale held by Bangs, Richard & Platt on August 26, 1839, calling it "a new and beautiful edition containing 9 more games than any other edition published in the United States, with handsome copper-plate frontispiece." The wholesale price was $2.00 per dozen books bound in boards with higher prices for embossed and roan bindings.
