Notes: The title page notes that this is translated from the thirteenth edition signed in the author's own hand. The chapters match through chapter 17, while chapters 18 through 20 are 19 through 21 in the original text. Chapter 21 combines the text of the original chapter 18 ("New Laws at Whist") and chapter 22 ("The Old Laws").

The appendix contains additional calculations at whist by de Moivre.
