Notes: In 1803, James Wallis sold a one-third share of The New Pocket Hoyle, second and all future editions to Mssrs. Vernor and Hood for eight guineas. The receipt is at the British Library, in the Collection of Assignments of Copyright Between English Authors and Publishers formed by Willaim Upcott, Add MS 38730 f. 195.

In the one copy I have seen in a slip case (O [Jessel f.621]), the slip case is dated 1802.
