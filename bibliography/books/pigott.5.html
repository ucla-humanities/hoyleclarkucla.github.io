Notes: This is a reprint with minor edits of the Pigott Hoyles, specifically Pigott.3.4, as it has some of the games that appear in the appendix there after the section on the Statute Laws of Gaming. Walker includes Loo, Lottery, Reversis and Speculation, while omitting Domino, Commerce, and Vingt-un.

Some copies (CSmH, NjP, Levy) retain the original printed cover showing a price of 3s.
