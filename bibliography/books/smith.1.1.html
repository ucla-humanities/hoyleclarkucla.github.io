Notes: This is the only dated version of Smith's New Pocket Hoyle. There are at least four undated versions, the same setting of type, certainly stereotyped, but with differences in the preliminary matter, pagination errors, and the colophon. Indeed the pagination errors differ among the copies of this dated edition.
