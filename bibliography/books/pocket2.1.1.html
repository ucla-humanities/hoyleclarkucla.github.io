Notices: 1808-07-27 Morning Post: "...A Companion to the Turf, the Billiard-Table, and the Card-Room.—This day is published, price 4.s 6d. in a case for the pocket; or in morocco, with a tuck to serve as a pocketbook 7s. bound; in morocco, with a silver lock, 9s. The New Pocket Hoyle, Part the Second...The above, with the first part, containing all the games on the cards, form the most copious and correct edition of Hoyle now extant, having been carefully revised by Charles Jackson, Esq. The treatise on the game of billiards having been greatly enlarged, and that on racing having been furnished with everything necessary to be known by any one attending the course, either as an amateur or for casual recreation."
