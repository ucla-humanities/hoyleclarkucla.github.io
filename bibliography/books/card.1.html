Notes: The book consists of all the card games from Pocket.1 with the exception of Reversis and Speculation. Whist and Quadrille are shortened; otherwise the text is identical, although the games are ordered differently. The material on Loo, Brag, Pope Joan, and Horse Racing are from Charles Jones' Hoyle's Games Improved, likely Jones.10 (1820). The material on horse racing, presented as an appendix at the end of the book, is shortened. The material on Commerce appears to be original, and is repeated in Smith.

The L and NvLN copies have a variant imprint and colophon. The imprint reads "AND SOLD BY BUMPUS..." leaving different subsequent line breaks. The colophon reads "Gough" rather than "Gough Square." The books are otherwise the same setting of type.
