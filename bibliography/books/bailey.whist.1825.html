Notes: The title page describes this as "Bailey's New Edition" with an address of 188, Fleet Street. The frontispiece shows an address of 66 Gray's Inn, suggesting that there may have been an earlier edition published from the Gray's Inn address.

Bailey apparently also published a Companion to the Card Table, but no copies have survived. See the note on B12v(48).
