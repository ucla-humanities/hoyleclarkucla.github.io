Backgammon, printed by T. Davison for R. Scholey et al, extracted from the first London edition, 1808.

Publisher: Robert Scholey, Vernor, Hood, and Sharpe, Lane and Newman, Thomas and Robert Hughes, Clement Chapple, Henry Delahoy Symonds, C. Booker, Scatcherd and Letterman, Wilson and Spence, Mundell and Co.
Printer: Thomas Davison
Notices: 1808-07-27 Morning Post: "...The game at billiards to be had separately, price 1s. 6d. Chess 1s. 6d. and Backgammon 1s... "
Price: 1s. (advertisement)
Notes: No copies survive.
