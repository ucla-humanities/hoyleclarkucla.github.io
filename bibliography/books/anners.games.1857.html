Notes: This book has two sets of printed signatures, alphabetic signatures for imposing in sixes, and numeric signature marks for imposing in eights. I am describing the book from a digital copy rather than a physical book, and chose to present the collation formula in sixes.

In the Anners edition, all pages are set within a double frame. Lippincott printed a number of impressions with the same setting of type, but without the double frame.
