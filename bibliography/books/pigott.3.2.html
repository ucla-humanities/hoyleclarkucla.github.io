Pigott.3.2

Pigott's New Hoyle, printed for J. Ridgway, third London edition, second issue, “tenth” edition, 1803.

Publisher: James Ridgway, Henry Delahoy Symonds (advertisement)
Printer: Thomas Sutton (colophon)
Notices: 1803-08-26 The Star
Price: 3s. (advertisement)
Notes: The only evidence for this "tenth" edition is the existence of a "ninth" edition and a "eleventh" which appear to be the same setting of type and the one newspaper advertisement mentioned above.
